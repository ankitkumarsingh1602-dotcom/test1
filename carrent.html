Safety guidelines

  • Always verify the driver's profile photo and vehicle number before boarding.
  • Share your live trip with a trusted contact.
  • Sit in the back seat on solo trips where possible.
  • Use the in-app SOS button in any emergency.
  • Never share OTPs or personal documents outside the platform.
  • Rate your co-passengers and drivers after every trip.
